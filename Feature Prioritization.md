| Feature | Priority | Business Impact | Effort |
|---------|----------|-----------------|--------|
| User Authentication | High | High | Medium |
| Task Creation | High | High | Low |
| Task Assignment | High | High | Medium |
| Due Date Reminders | High | High | Low |
| Team Collaboration | High | High | High |
| Project Dashboard | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Progress Tracking | Medium | High | Medium |
| Notifications | Medium | Medium | Low |
| Analytics Dashboard | Low | Medium | High |
| AI Task Suggestions | Low | High | High |
