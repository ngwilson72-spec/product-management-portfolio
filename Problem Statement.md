## Objective
Clearly define the problem that the product aims to solve.

## Problem

Many professionals and students struggle to manage tasks, deadlines, and team collaboration because they rely on multiple disconnected tools. This leads to missed deadlines, poor communication, and reduced productivity.

## Impact
- Lower productivity
- Missed deadlines
- Poor collaboration
- Increased stress

## Proposed Solution
Develop a centralized product management platform that helps users organize tasks, collaborate in real time, track progress, and meet deadlines efficiently.

## Success Metrics
- 30% reduction in missed deadlines
- 25% increase in task completion rate
- Improved user satisfaction
- Higher daily active user engagement

## Deliverable
A well-defined problem statement with a proposed solution and measurable success metrics.
